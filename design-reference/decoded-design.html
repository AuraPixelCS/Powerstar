FAQ

The questions shippers actually ask.

The VGM is the total confirmed gross weight of a packed container — cargo plus tare. It's mandated under the SOLAS convention: a container will not be loaded onto a vessel without a verified VGM. We guide you through obtaining and submitting it correctly, keeping your shipment safe, compliant and free of demurrage charges.
A Harmonized System code is the international language for classifying traded goods. It determines your duties and taxes, ensures smooth customs clearance, and triggers any required permits. We classify your goods accurately to avoid delays, inspections and costly misclassification penalties.
Carrier liability is strictly limited by international treaty and covers only a fraction of your cargo's value. Cargo insurance is an all-risk safety net against natural disasters, water damage, theft and handling accidents — protecting your cash flow and your customer relationships. We arrange comprehensive cover tailored to your shipment's value and route.
We work with you to classify goods, prepare documentation and optimise duty costs — handling the complexity end-to-end so your shipment stays compliant, on time and protected.
